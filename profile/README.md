# CoralMind

CoralMind helps organizations understand, safeguard against, and improve AI's impact on people's mental wellbeing and cognitive functioning.

## Repositories

| Repository | What it is |
|---|---|
| [knowledge-library](https://github.com/coralmind/knowledge-library) | A structured account of how AI systems contribute to harm in mental health contexts: pathways, behaviors, impacts, and the evidence behind each claim |
| [toolkit](https://github.com/coralmind/toolkit) | Open-source tooling to simulate and judge mental-health AI evaluations against the Knowledge Library |

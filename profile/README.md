# CoralMind

CoralMind helps organizations better understand, safeguard against, and improve AI's impact on people's mental wellbeing and cognitive functioning.

> **Content warning:** these repositories describe harm in mental health contexts, including suicide, self-harm, and psychiatric crisis. CoralMind is not a crisis service. If you need help now, contact local emergency services. In the United States, call or text **988** (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline), or chat at [988lifeline.org](https://988lifeline.org). Outside the United States, [findahelpline.com](https://findahelpline.com) lists free, confidential services by country.

## Repositories

These are the open-source parts of CoralMind's [field infrastructure](https://coralmind.org/our-work).

| Repository | What it is |
|---|---|
| [knowledge-library](https://github.com/coralmind/knowledge-library) | Synthesizes what is known, how strong the evidence is, where findings agree or conflict, and what important gaps remain |
| [toolkit](https://github.com/coralmind/toolkit) | Connects methods, measures, frameworks, tools, and technical capabilities that support research and evaluation. Its `data-repository/` holds the test cases, personas, and run output |

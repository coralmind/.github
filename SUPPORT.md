# Support

CoralMind conducts research and builds field infrastructure on AI's impact on people's mental wellbeing and cognitive functioning. It is not a clinical service, not a crisis service, and not a medical device. Nothing in these repositories is medical advice or a substitute for care from a qualified professional.

## If you are in crisis

If you are thinking about suicide, or you are otherwise in crisis, contact local emergency services.

- In the United States, call or text **988** (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline), or chat at [988lifeline.org](https://988lifeline.org).
- Outside the United States, [findahelpline.com](https://findahelpline.com) lists free, confidential services by country.

Please do not use GitHub issues to ask for personal mental health support. The people who maintain these repositories cannot provide it.

## Questions and Getting Involved

Contribution guidelines are still being written. For a question about the projects, or to talk about getting involved, use the contact form:

**[Contact us](https://coralmind.org/get-involved?sourceId=GitHub&involve=other#connect)**

## Problems and Ideas

Use the issue templates in the repository: a bug report for code, a content report for content, or a feature request. Each project's README says what to report there and what to raise privately instead. Do not include real patient data, identifiable crisis narratives, or other personal information.

## Security and Safety-Sensitive Content

See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Do not open a public issue.

# Support

CoralMind publishes research material and evaluation tools. It is not a clinical service, not a crisis service, and not a medical device. Nothing in these repositories is medical advice or a substitute for care from a qualified professional.

## If you are in crisis

If you are thinking about suicide, or you are otherwise in crisis, contact local emergency services.

- In the United States, call or text **988** (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline), or chat at [988lifeline.org](https://988lifeline.org).
- Outside the United States, [findahelpline.com](https://findahelpline.com) lists free, confidential services by country.

Please do not use GitHub issues to ask for personal mental health support. The people who maintain these repositories cannot provide it.

Use the issue templates in the repository. Do not include real patient data, identifiable crisis narratives, or other personal information.
